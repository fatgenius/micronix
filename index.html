Frequently asked questions

What is NIX?

NIX is a compact water-treatment system that combines ultrasound-assisted microplastic separation with water-quality monitoring.

How does NIX work?

Ultrasonic treatment promotes the aggregation and settling of microplastic particles. Upper-layer water leaves through a separate outlet, while concentrated particles flow through the lower outlet into a collection filter.

Does NIX remove all microplastics?

No. NIX is designed to reduce microplastic presence, not guarantee complete removal. Results vary with particle size and type, concentration, and water conditions.

What does NIX monitor?

NIX tracks pH, temperature, TDS, and turbidity. Its software brings readings, alerts, history, reports, device control, and maintenance reminders together.

Does NIX measure microplastic concentration directly?

No. TDS and turbidity do not directly measure microplastic concentration. Microplastic performance is evaluated separately through appropriate testing.

Where can NIX be used?

Aquaculture is the primary application. The compact system may also be adapted to selected industrial, public, and general water-management settings.

What maintenance does NIX require?

Routine care includes cleaning the particle-collection area, checking sensors, replacing filters, and inspecting system operation.
